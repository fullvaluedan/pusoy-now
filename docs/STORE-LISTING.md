## Full description (both stores)

Prends is the fast, friendly way to play Pusoy Dos, the Filipino card game also known as Big Two.

Deal in and empty your hand first. Play singles, pairs, and five-card hands like straights, flushes, and full houses. The 2 of diamonds is the highest card in the deck.

WHAT YOU GET
- Play instantly as a guest, no sign-up needed.
- Three bot difficulties: Easy, Normal, and Expert that actually think.
- Quick Match: get seated against real players in seconds, with bots filling empty seats.
- Private rooms: start a table and invite friends with a link.
- Add friends and track your head-to-head record against each of them.
- Clean, chunky, easy-to-read table designed for one-handed phone play.

WHY PRENDS
- No ads.
- No real-money gambling.
- No tracking.
- Free.

Sign in with Apple, Google, Facebook, or email to save your stats, pick a username, and keep your progress across devices. Your guest progress carries over when you sign in.
